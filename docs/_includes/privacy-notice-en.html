Website and hosting

This static website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Recipients of technical access data are GitHub and the service providers used to operate its hosting infrastructure. GitHub identifies GitHub, Inc. in the United States and GitHub B.V. in the Netherlands in its privacy statement.

When you request a page, GitHub receives the technical data needed to deliver it. This includes your IP address, the requested page and, depending on the request, the date and time, browser and device details and the previously visited page (referrer). GitHub expressly states that website visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

The processing serves to provide and secure this website. The legal basis for the processing initiated by me is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is a reliable and secure website. GitHub determines how long technical logs are retained; the linked privacy information does not specify a fixed retention period specifically for GitHub Pages access logs.

GitHub also processes data in the United States and other countries. For international transfers, GitHub identifies safeguards including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Details about recipients, transfers, safeguards and retention are available in the GitHub privacy statement.

No analytics or marketing services

This website does not embed analytics or marketing services and does not itself set cookies or use local storage or session storage. Fonts, images and platform logos are delivered with the website rather than loaded from external font or widget providers.

There is no contact form, user account or embedded social media widget. A link to another website does not by itself transfer data from this website to its destination. Only when you follow a link does the destination provider process data under its own privacy information. Email and phone links open the corresponding application on your device.

Email and phone enquiries

When you contact me, I process the information you provide, such as your name, email address or phone number and the content of your enquiry. The purpose is to handle and respond to your enquiry. Information for general enquiries is voluntary; without the contact details needed to respond, I may be unable to reply. Contract-related information may be necessary to enter into or perform a contract.

Where an enquiry concerns entering into or performing a contract with you, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other enquiries, it is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is handling incoming enquiries.

I use only Proton Mail, operated by Proton AG, Route de la Galaise 32, 1228 Plan-les-Ouates, Geneva, Switzerland, to receive, send and store email. For delivery and security, Proton also processes email metadata such as sender, recipient, subject, timestamps and the sending server's IP address. Email from external providers is not automatically end-to-end encrypted; Proton may scan unencrypted incoming messages for spam and malware before storing them in encrypted form.

Proton states that Proton Mail data is stored encrypted in Switzerland, Germany or Norway. Switzerland is covered by a European Commission adequacy decision; Germany and Norway are part of the EU and EEA respectively. Further information is available in the Proton Mail privacy policy and the European Commission's information on adequacy decisions.

For phone calls, the telecommunications providers involved also process the data necessary to establish and handle the connection under their own privacy information.

Enquiry retention

General enquiries are deleted once they have been fully resolved and no further retention is required for contract handling, statutory retention duties or establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Contract and billing records are retained in accordance with the applicable statutory duties. The legal basis for mandatory statutory retention is Article 6(1)(c) GDPR; for necessary retention relating to legal claims, it is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Proton specifies a retention period of up to 30 days for encrypted backups. GitHub's retention of technical access data is described in the hosting section.

Your rights

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have rights of access to your personal data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Articles 15 to 20 GDPR). You can use the contact details above to exercise your rights.

Objecting to processing

Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object on grounds relating to your particular situation under Article 21 GDPR. Processing will then stop unless compelling legitimate grounds are demonstrated that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Complaining to a supervisory authority

Under Article 77 GDPR, you can complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the place of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. The Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA) is responsible for private-sector organisations in Bavaria. Complaint information is available from BayLDA.

No automated decisions
